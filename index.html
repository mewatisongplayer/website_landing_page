About this app

Mewati Song Player is a music app for Mewati folk songs. Browse songs and singers, download your favorites and listen offline.

Features

  • Offline downloads. Save songs and play them without internet.
  • Equalizer and Mewati Bass. Adjust the sound to your taste.
  • Drive mode. Big buttons and a simple screen while driving.
  • Sleep timer. Music stops by itself at the time you set.
  • Voice search. Say a song name to find it.
  • Themes. Cyber Black, Silver Chrome and Walkman Orange.
